### 5. Pricing & Packages Policy

- Do not hardcode prices into AI prompts, messages, templates or application logic.
- Do not invent prices, discounts, package names, guarantees or deliverables.
- Pricing can change over time and depends on the client's requirements.
- Do not quote a price unless an authorized user has provided or approved the current price.
- If a prospect asks for pricing, generate a polite reply explaining that pricing depends on the required services and scope.
- Ask the prospect about their requirements or flag the conversation for human review.
- Keep service descriptions, deliverables and pricing in editable settings so an administrator can update them without changing code.

Suggested reply to a pricing question:

"Thank you for your interest! Our pricing depends on the services and requirements of your business. Could you share a little more about what you're looking for? We can then suggest a suitable solution."

### 6. AI Personalization Rules

When researching a business:

- Use only legitimately available public information or approved data sources.
- Recommend services based on relevant evidence.
- Never invent facts about a business.
- Never claim that a business has a problem without sufficient evidence.
- Do not make negative or insulting comments about its current marketing.
- Avoid generic, repetitive and aggressive sales messages.
- Do not guarantee sales, leads, viral reach or business growth.
- Do not claim previous contact, familiarity or a partnership unless verified.
- Keep outreach messages concise, natural, professional and personalized.
- Use the correct business name and relevant service.
- Do not include pricing unless authorized.
- Respect opt-outs and do-not-contact requests.

### 7. Outreach Objective

The objective is to start a relevant business conversation, understand the prospect's needs and identify potential clients for ProSolution Infotech.

The AI should not force a sale. It should recommend relevant services, draft suitable messages and route interested prospects to a human team member.

### 8. Human Approval

Human approval is required by default before outreach messages are sent.

The system must support:
- Edit message
- Approve message
- Skip lead
- Mark do not contact
- Escalate interested prospects
- Review pricing requests

Actual Instagram messaging must use supported official integrations and permissions. If a requested outbound messaging action is unavailable, the system must offer a compliant manual workflow instead.
